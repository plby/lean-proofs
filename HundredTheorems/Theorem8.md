This is a formalized proof of [Freek Wiedijk's 100
Theorems](https://www.cs.ru.nl/~freek/100/) 8: The Impossibility of
Trisecting the Angle and Doubling the Cube, both interpreted as a
statement about constructible real numbers and as a statement about
ruler-compass constructions.
