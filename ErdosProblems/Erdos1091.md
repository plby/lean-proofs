This is a formalized proof of [Erdős Problem 1091](https://www.erdosproblems.com/forum/thread/1091).

Every four-chromatic, `K₄`-free finite graph has an odd cycle with at least two chords, but no growing quantitative guarantee exists; explicit four-critical counterexamples are also constructed.
