This is a formalized proof of [Erdős Problem 207](https://www.erdosproblems.com/forum/thread/207).

It proves the existence of high-girth Steiner triple systems for all sufficiently large admissible orders.

It is available for these Mathlib (and Lean) versions:

* [Mathlib/Lean v4.33.0](../src/latest/ErdosProblems/Erdos207.lean) (latest stable release).
