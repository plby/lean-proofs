This is a formalized proof of [Erdős Problem
707](https://www.erdosproblems.com/forum/thread/707).

For more information, see [this paper](https://borisalexeev.com/papers/erdos707.html):
"Forbidden Sidon subsets of perfect difference sets, featuring a human-assisted proof"
by Boris Alexeev, ChatGPT, Lean, and Dustin G. Mixon
