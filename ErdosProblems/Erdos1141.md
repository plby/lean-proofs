This is a formalized proof of [Erdős Problem 1141](https://www.erdosproblems.com/forum/thread/1141).

The latest proof is unconditional. It replaces the assumed Pollack theorem with a proved
small-prime existence result, using a weak Burgess estimate and a Siegel lower bound for
quadratic L-values. The final theorem statements are unchanged, and their only axioms are
`propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound`. The older version snapshots are unchanged.

It is available for these Mathlib (and Lean) versions:

* [Mathlib/Lean v4.33.0](../src/latest/ErdosProblems/Erdos1141.lean) (latest stable release).
* [Mathlib/Lean v4.32.0](../src/v4.32.0/ErdosProblems/Erdos1141.lean).
