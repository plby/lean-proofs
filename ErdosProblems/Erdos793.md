## Statement and authorship

`Erdos793.erdos_793` proves that
`(F(n) - π(n)) / (n^(2/3)/(log n)²)` tends to `27/2`.
`F(n)` is the maximum cardinality of a subset of `{1, …, n}` such that no
member `a` divides `b*c` when `a ≠ b` and `a ≠ c`. The definition deliberately
allows `b = c`, as in EPC's statement.

**GPT-5.6 Sol Ultra**, prompted by **Przemek Chojecki**, supplied the refined
argument. As Thomas Bloom explains in EPC comment 7599, the upper bound
tracks the constant in Erdős's 1938 proof; the lower bound refines his
prime-triple construction to attain the matching constant.
The exact constant, rather than only existence of some constant, is proved.

**Wouter van Doorn** (`Woett`) posted the formalization generated by
**Aristotle**. **Jake Mallen** subsequently integrated a proved PNT dependency
in the complete collection used here. The metadata includes both human
contributors while distinguishing proof generation and dependency integration.

## Port and Comparator

The proof is placed in namespace `Erdos793` and split into `Definitions`,
`Upper`, and `Lower`. The final theorem discharges the `PNT` parameter with
the actual PNT+ theorem, without declaring an axiom.
The independent Comparator challenge imports only Mathlib and repeats only
the strongly-two-primitive predicate, the extremal function, and the final
limit assertion.

The port replaces ambiguous limit conversions with explicit limit operations,
normalizes division and real powers, expands the finite-union count directly,
and makes the prime-bin cardinalities explicit. Classical instances remain
local, and source tactic-style warnings have been removed.
