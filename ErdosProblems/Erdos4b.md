This is a formalized proof of [Erdős Problem 4](https://www.erdosproblems.com/forum/thread/4).

The development also proves the stronger FGKMT18 bound, both below every
sufficiently large endpoint and for infinitely many consecutive-prime indices.

It is available for these Mathlib (and Lean) versions:

* [Mathlib/Lean v4.33.0](../src/latest/ErdosProblems/Erdos4b.lean) (latest stable release).
