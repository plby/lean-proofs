This is a formalized proof of [Erdős Problem 577](https://www.erdosproblems.com/577).

Every finite simple graph on `4 * k` vertices with minimum degree at least
`2 * k` contains `k` pairwise vertex-disjoint cycles of exactly length four.
The cycles need not be induced, and the statement includes `k = 0` and `k = 1`.
