This is a formalized proof of [Erdős Problem 1010](https://www.erdosproblems.com/forum/thread/1010).

Every simple graph on `n` vertices with `⌊n²/4⌋ + t` edges, where `t < ⌊n/2⌋`, contains at least `t⌊n/2⌋` triangles.
